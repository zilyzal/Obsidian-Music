## Obsidian Music 1.3

A new liquid-glass look, with the player features that go with it.

**New**
- Real glass: the mini player, dock and player controls refract and blur what moves behind them (Android 13 and up; older phones get a frosted look).
- Four tabs: Home, Tracks, Albums and Search. Latest, Favorites, Oldest and Longest now live on the Tracks tab.
- A living background that drifts and takes its colours from the cover.
- Waveform seek bar, 3D swipe-to-skip artwork, swipe the mini player to skip.
- In the full player: Up Next queue, a 5-band equalizer with presets, a soundstage toggle and a sleep timer.
- Repeat button (off, all, one) and a Recently Played row.
- Liquid / Frosted switch on Home if you prefer a calmer look.

**Notes**
- Lyrics screen is in place but has nothing to show yet.
- Builds are signed with a debug key, so Android may refuse to install this over an older version. If so, uninstall first (starred songs reset).

Download: `ObsidianMusic.apk` below.
