## Obsidian Music 1.4

A full redesign on top of 1.2: true liquid glass and fluid motion everywhere.

**True liquid glass**
- Floating controls now really refract what is behind them: lens bending at the edges, light blur, colour vibrancy and a specular rim (Android 13+). Older phones get frosted obsidian glass.
- Glass swells like a drop of liquid under your finger, with a light haptic tick.
- The top bar floats above the list. Songs scroll under it and fade out before they reach it, so no text clashes with the glass.

**Player**
- The cover flies from the mini player into the full player and back.
- The background is the cover itself, blown up and blurred, so the player is washed in the song's colours.
- Swipe the cover sideways to skip. It tilts with your finger and flies off.
- New seek bar: a clear glass thumb that magnifies the bar under it while you drag.
- Thick glass play button, skip arrows that nudge, a star that spins and glows.

**New features**
- **Search**: the search lens stretches into a glass field. It searches titles, artists and albums in the current view.
- **Up Next**: see what plays next (follows shuffle). Tap a song to jump to it.
- **Play next**: long-press any song in the list.
- **Repeat one**: toggle it in the player.
- **Sleep timer**: 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or the end of this song.
- **Mini player swipe**: swipe it sideways to skip.

**Motion**
- Views cross-dissolve when you switch them, the menu springs out of the pill with rows cascading in, and play/pause morphs with a spring.
- Nothing animates in the background, so it stays light on battery.

**Install note**
Builds are signed with a debug key that may not match earlier versions. If Android refuses to install over an old version, uninstall it first (your starred songs will reset).
