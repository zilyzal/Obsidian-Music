## Obsidian Music 1.1

A new look across the whole app, same simple player.

**New**
- Redesigned interface: refined glass, Sora and Manrope type, glass shards in the background, glow colours that follow the album art.
- Dropdown menu now dims the screen, with a raised pill and an icon for each view.
- Mini player with the song's progress drawn as a ring around play/pause.
- Full player shows where the song is playing from (Latest, Favorites, ...), with a larger seek bar and glass play button.
- First-launch screen that explains what access is asked for and why.
- Friendlier empty states for Favorites and for a phone with no music.

**Faster and lighter**
- Row artwork is decoded at list size instead of full size, so scrolling long libraries is smoother and uses less memory.
- Accent colour is worked out only for the playing song, not for every row.
- Player events no longer scan the whole library.

**Install note**
Builds are signed with a debug key that is not guaranteed to match 1.0.0, so Android may refuse to install this over it. If that happens, uninstall 1.0.0 first (your starred songs will reset).

Download: `ObsidianMusic.apk` below.
